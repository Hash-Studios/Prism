# Prism Wallpapers

Prism Wallpapers is a wallpaper discovery and personalization app for Android and iOS, built by Hash Studios. Find high-quality images and curated collections for your phone, explore visual categories and popular picks, and make your home screen feel more personal.

Browse [wallpapers](/wallpapers), [desktop wallpapers](/desktop-wallpapers), [popular wallpapers](/wallpapers/popular), and [categories](/categories). Prism features curated discovery, high-resolution artwork, dark and AMOLED-friendly styles, and a personal library for saved favorites.

## Common questions

### What is Prism Wallpapers?
Prism is a wallpaper app for Android and iOS with curated collections and high-resolution images.

### Can I browse by style or device?
Yes. Explore popular picks, categories, phone wallpapers, and desktop wallpapers.

### Is Prism free?
Prism is free to download. Optional premium features and subscription details are listed on the [pricing page](/pricing.md).

### Can developers and agents integrate?
Yes. Read the [API documentation](/docs), [developer guide](/developers), and [MCP guide](/mcp).
