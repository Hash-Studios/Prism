# About Prism Wallpapers

Prism is a wallpaper discovery and personalization app built by Hash Studios for Android and iOS. It brings high-quality images and curated collections together so people can find a background that suits their screen and style. Explore 4K artwork, dark AMOLED-friendly designs, nature, abstract art, minimal looks, and more.

Prism aims to make discovery feel inspiring rather than overwhelming. Browse by visual theme, mood, subject, or popular picks; preview artwork; save favorites; and return when you are ready to personalize your device. The app supports everyday discovery across phone screens and larger displays, with clear paths into collections and categories.

Hash Studios improves Prism through app updates and community feedback. The project is open source, and developers can learn about the product, explore integration options, or contribute through the public repository. Read the [developer resources](/developers), [API documentation](/docs), or [contact the team](/contact).
