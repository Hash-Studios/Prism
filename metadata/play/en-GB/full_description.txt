Prism is a wallpaper app for people who care how their phone looks. Find a wall you love, make your own with AI, and set it on your home screen, lock screen or both in a tap.

New in Prism

• Folders. Save walls into private folders, make a folder public, or follow someone else's.
• Live wallpapers on supported phones. Make a wall live, try a living gradient or the 3D style, or turn a video into a wallpaper.
• Auto-rotate. With Pro, Prism changes your wallpaper on a schedule, straight from a folder.
• 3D preview. Tilt your phone to see a wall's depth before you set it.
• AI styles and remix. Describe an idea, see it in every style, then remix the AI walls you like.
• Weekly challenges. Enter the theme of the week and vote for your favourites. The winner becomes the Wall of the Day.
• Creators. Follow the artists you like and see their new walls in your Following feed.

4K and AMOLED walls

• Sharp 4K walls and true-black AMOLED backgrounds
• A new Wall of the Day every morning
• Browse by category and mood, or search by word, colour or tag
• A feed that learns what you like
• Preview any wall on a real Home Screen and Lock Screen first
• Set a wall on your home screen, lock screen or both, and undo in a tap

Make your own with AI

Type an idea, pick a style from anime to oil painting, and Prism creates a wallpaper from your idea. Ask for another take, refine it with a follow-up, or remix a wall someone else made. AI wallpapers cost coins, and you can earn coins in the app.

Your library

• Favourites, Folders, Downloads and History in one place
• Pin up to 12 favourites
• Your history follows you to a new phone when you sign in

Made with the community

• Upload your own walls. Every upload is reviewed before it goes live
• Ask for a wallpaper and vote on other people's requests
• Share a wall as a story, a post or a clean card, or send it to Instagram Stories
• Report content or block users at any time

Make it yours

• Light and dark themes, AMOLED black and your own accent colour
• A Material You theme that follows your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and newer
• A Wall of the Day home screen widget with one-tap Set
