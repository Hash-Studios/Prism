# Prism Wallpapers pricing

Prism is free to download on Android and iOS. The free experience supports wallpaper discovery and browsing. Optional Prism Premium features are offered through in-app purchases; exact availability, price, billing period, and local currency can vary by platform and storefront. Check the purchase screen in the app for current price and terms before subscribing. The public pricing endpoint lists the tiers only, with no prices.

## Plans and features

- **Free:** Download the app and explore available wallpaper discovery features.
- **Prism Premium:** Optional paid access to premium features and benefits shown in the app at the time of purchase.

Subscriptions are billed and managed by the relevant app store. Review the plan details and renewal terms before confirming a purchase. Do not rely on a hard-coded price copied from an older page; storefront pricing can change by region and over time. For integration use, see the pricing endpoint in the [API documentation](/docs) or the [MCP server](/mcp).
