# Contact Prism Wallpapers

We welcome product questions, feedback, bug reports, partnership notes, and ideas that can improve Prism. Contact Hash Studios by email at [hash.studios.inc@gmail.com](mailto:hash.studios.inc@gmail.com). For account or purchase issues, include the platform and a clear description, but never send passwords, authentication codes, or payment details.

- Telegram: [Prism Wallpapers community](https://t.me/PrismWallpapers)
- GitHub: [Hash-Studios/Prism](https://github.com/Hash-Studios/Prism) for source issues and contributions.
- X: [@PrismWallpapers](https://x.com/PrismWallpapers) for public news and announcements.

For content or copyright concerns, identify the relevant material and explain the issue. Developers can review the [API documentation](/docs) before contacting us about integration behavior.
